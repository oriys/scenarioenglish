# Scene 001 — 机场柜台值机 / Counter Check-in

> **Path:** 机场与飞行 → 值机 → 柜台值机  
> **Region:** UK-first / General English  
> **Target level:** A2–B1 production, real-world input may be harder

## 1. 场景介绍

你到达机场后，第一件高概率需要独立完成的事，就是找到航空公司的值机柜台，出示证件、确认航班、托运行李、选择座位，并拿到登机牌。

这个场景看起来流程固定，但真实交流里经常会出现：行李是否托运、是否有危险品、是否愿意坐紧急出口座位、行李超重、证件确认、转机信息、座位偏好等问题。

**本场景成功结果：**你能独立完成柜台值机，并在出现一个常见异常时继续把流程办完。

## 2. 任务目标

完成本场景后，你应该能：

- 主动说明“我要办理这个航班的值机”；
- 听懂工作人员对护照、行李、座位和目的地的常见提问；
- 清楚回答是否有托运行李、是否自己打包、是否携带危险品；
- 提出靠窗、靠过道、相邻座位等需求；
- 听不懂时主动要求重复、放慢或确认数字；
- 遇到行李超重时询问费用和替代方案；
- 最终确认登机口、登机时间和是否需要再次托运行李。

## 3. 正常流程

1. 找到航空公司对应航班的 `Check-in` 柜台。
2. 排队，到柜台后先打招呼。
3. 出示护照，必要时出示预订信息。
4. 工作人员确认目的地和航班。
5. 工作人员询问是否有托运行李。
6. 将托运行李放到行李秤/传送带上。
7. 回答安全问题，例如行李是否由本人打包。
8. 如有需要，提出座位偏好。
9. 工作人员打印登机牌和行李标签。
10. 确认登机口、登机时间和后续流程。

## 4. 常见异常

- **行李超重**：需要付费、重新打包，或把部分物品放进随身行李。
- **座位不理想**：没有靠窗/靠过道，或同行者没坐在一起。
- **预订信息找不到**：姓名拼写、航班号、护照信息可能需要重新核对。
- **联程航班**：需要确认行李是否直挂最终目的地。
- **航班接近关闭值机**：工作人员可能催促你尽快去安检和登机口。

## 5. 加分场景

如果你想把这个场景练到“旅行中比较从容”的程度，还要会：

- 询问能否换到靠前的位置；
- 询问空余座位；
- 确认行李在转机地是否需要重新托运；
- 确认登机牌上 `boarding time` 与 `departure time` 的区别；
- 在工作人员语速快时，不慌张地修复对话。

## 8. 你应该说的话

1. **Hi, I'd like to check in for my flight to London.** — 我想办理去伦敦航班的值机。
2. **Sure. Here's my passport.** — 好的，这是我的护照。
3. **I have one bag to check in.** — 我有一件托运行李。
4. **I only have cabin baggage.** — 我只有随身行李。
5. **Yes, I packed it myself.** — 是的，我自己打包的。
6. **No, nobody gave me anything to carry.** — 没有人让我帮忙携带物品。
7. **Could I have a window seat, please?** — 可以给我靠窗座位吗？
8. **An aisle seat would be better, if possible.** — 如果可以，我更想要靠过道。
9. **Is there any seat closer to the front?** — 有更靠前的座位吗？
10. **How much is the excess baggage charge?** — 超重费用是多少？
11. **Can I take something out and weigh it again?** — 我可以拿一些东西出来再称一次吗？
12. **Is my bag checked through to my final destination?** — 我的行李会直挂到最终目的地吗？
13. **Do I need to collect my bag during the connection?** — 转机时我需要取行李吗？
14. **What time does boarding start?** — 几点开始登机？
15. **Which gate should I go to?** — 我应该去哪个登机口？
16. **Sorry, did you say Gate 42?** — 抱歉，您说的是 42 号登机口吗？
17. **Could you say that again a little more slowly, please?** — 能再慢一点说一遍吗？

## 10. 10 个可迁移核心句型

### Pattern 1 — 说明自己要办理什么

`I'd like to + 动作.`

> I'd like to check in for my flight to London.

可迁移：酒店入住、买票、预约、退货。

### Pattern 2 — 表示自己有多少物品

`I have + 数量 + 名词 + to + 动作.`

> I have one bag to check in.

### Pattern 3 — 礼貌提出需求

`Could I have + 名词, please?`

> Could I have a window seat, please?

可迁移：餐厅、酒店、咖啡店、租车。

### Pattern 4 — 询问是否有更好的替代

`Is there any + 名词 + 比较级/补充条件?`

> Is there any seat closer to the front?

### Pattern 5 — 条件式偏好

`I'd prefer + 名词, if possible.`

> I'd prefer an aisle seat, if possible.

### Pattern 6 — 询问费用

`How much is the + 费用名称?`

> How much is the excess baggage charge?

### Pattern 7 — 询问是否需要做某件事

`Do I need to + 动作?`

> Do I need to collect my bag in London?

可迁移性极高。

### Pattern 8 — 确认信息

`Did you say + 信息?`

> Sorry, did you say Gate 42?

数字、时间、地址、车站都能用。

### Pattern 9 — 请求重复/降速

`Could you + 动作 + a little more slowly, please?`

> Could you say that again a little more slowly, please?

### Pattern 10 — 确认最终状态

`Is + 名词 + 过去分词 + to + 地点?`

> Is my bag checked through to Manchester?

用于确认行李、订单、预订、包裹等状态。

## 11. 完整真实角色扮演（44 turns）

> 情境：你从亚洲出发，经伦敦转机去爱丁堡。你有一件托运行李，行李略微超重，并希望要靠过道的位置。工作人员语速正常，中间你有一次没听清。

**Staff:** Good morning. Where are you flying to today?  
**You:** Hi. I'm flying to Edinburgh via London.

**Staff:** Great. May I see your passport, please?  
**You:** Sure. Here you are.

**Staff:** Thank you. Do you have your booking reference as well?  
**You:** Yes. It's on my phone. One moment, please.

**Staff:** That's fine. I've found your booking. Are you checking in any bags today?  
**You:** Yes, I have one bag to check in.

**Staff:** Could you put it on the scale for me, please?  
**You:** Of course.

**Staff:** Thank you. Did you pack this bag yourself?  
**You:** Yes, I did.

**Staff:** Has anyone given you anything to carry for them?  
**You:** No, nobody has.

**Staff:** And do you have any spare lithium batteries or power banks in the checked bag?  
**You:** Sorry, could you say that again a little more slowly, please?

**Staff:** Of course. Do you have any power banks or spare batteries inside this bag?  
**You:** No. My power bank is in my cabin bag.

**Staff:** Perfect. Your checked bag is 24.2 kilos, and your allowance is 23 kilos.  
**You:** Sorry, did you say 24.2 kilos?

**Staff:** Yes. You're 1.2 kilos over the limit.  
**You:** I see. How much is the excess baggage charge?

**Staff:** It would be £65. Alternatively, you can take something out and we'll weigh the bag again.  
**You:** I'd rather take something out. Is that okay?

**Staff:** Absolutely. You can step to the side and repack it.  
**You:** Thanks. I'll take out my camera charger and a jacket.

**Staff:** No problem. Let me know when you're ready.  
**You:** Okay, could we weigh it again now?

**Staff:** Sure. It's 22.8 kilos now, so that's fine.  
**You:** Great, thank you.

**Staff:** Now, would you prefer a window or an aisle seat?  
**You:** I'd prefer an aisle seat, if possible.

**Staff:** I have 31C available from here to London. For London to Edinburgh, I can give you 18D.  
**You:** Is there any aisle seat closer to the front on the first flight?

**Staff:** Let me check. I also have 14C, but it's near the toilets.  
**You:** In that case, 31C is fine.

**Staff:** All right. I've assigned 31C and 18D.  
**You:** Thank you. Is my bag checked through to Edinburgh?

**Staff:** Yes, it is. You don't need to collect it in London.  
**You:** So I can go straight to my connecting flight after immigration and security?

**Staff:** That's right. Just follow the purple Flight Connections signs when you arrive at Heathrow.  
**You:** Sorry, did you say the purple signs?

**Staff:** Yes, the purple Flight Connections signs.  
**You:** Got it.

**Staff:** Here are your two boarding passes and your baggage receipt.  
**You:** Thanks. What time does boarding start for the first flight?

**Staff:** Boarding starts at 10:20, and the gate closes at 10:55.  
**You:** And which gate should I go to?

**Staff:** Gate 36. It's about a 15-minute walk after security.  
**You:** Okay. Gate 36, boarding at 10:20.

**Staff:** Exactly. I'd recommend going straight through security now.  
**You:** I will. Thank you for your help.

**Staff:** You're welcome. Have a good flight.  
**You:** Thank you. Have a good day.

## 12. 听不懂时的万能应对句

下面这些不是“尴尬补救”，而是旅行中必须主动掌握的对话修复工具。

### 请求重复

- **Sorry, could you say that again?**
- **Sorry, I didn't catch that.**

### 请求慢一点

- **Could you say that a little more slowly, please?**
- **Would you mind speaking a bit more slowly?**

### 只确认关键内容

- **Sorry, did you say 23 kilos?**
- **Did you say Gate 36?**
- **Was that 10:20 or 10:30?**

### 不认识某个词

- **Sorry, what does “allowance” mean?**
- **Could you say that in a different way?**

### 让对方通过视觉帮助你

- **Could you show me on the boarding pass?**
- **Could you write that down for me, please?**
- **Could you point to it on the screen?**

> 实战原则：不要试图听懂整句。先抓 `bag / gate / time / passport / seat / London` 这种关键词，再确认核心信息。

## 14. 易错点

### 易错 1：把“办理登机”直译成 do check-in

❌ **I want to do check-in.**  
✅ **I'd like to check in.**

`check in` 本身就是动词短语。

### 易错 2：混淆 passport 与 boarding pass

- `passport` = 护照
- `boarding pass` = 登机牌

工作人员说 **May I see your passport?** 时，不要只递手机里的登机牌。

### 易错 3：baggage / luggage 的数量表达

❌ **two luggages**  
✅ **two bags**  
✅ **two pieces of luggage**

旅行口语里直接说 `one bag / two bags` 最自然、最简单。

### 易错 4：aisle 的发音

`aisle` 读音接近 **I'll**，字母 `s` 不发音。

### 易错 5：只说 window

能听懂，但完整表达更自然：

✅ **Could I have a window seat, please?**

### 易错 6：没听懂时只说 What?

`What?` 在很多情境下显得突然。

优先：

✅ **Sorry, could you say that again?**

### 易错 7：把 boarding time 当成 departure time

- `boarding time` = 开始登机时间
- `departure time` = 计划起飞时间
- `gate closes` = 登机口关闭时间

旅行时最重要的是**不要只盯起飞时间**。

## 15. 扩展说明

### 15.1 为什么工作人员反复问行李问题？

这些通常属于航空安全流程。你只需要简短、准确回答，不需要解释很长。

### 15.2 power bank 通常应该放哪里？

航空公司通常要求充电宝、备用锂电池随身携带而不是放入托运行李。具体限制应以航空公司和机场当日规定为准。

英语重点不是背规定，而是能听懂：

- **Do you have any power banks in your checked baggage?**
- **You'll need to remove that and put it in your cabin bag.**

### 15.3 “checked through” 是非常值得掌握的表达

如果你有转机，最关键的问题之一是：

**Is my bag checked through to my final destination?**

如果答案是 yes，你通常不需要在中转机场主动取行李；如果工作人员说：

**You'll need to collect and recheck your bag.**

就意味着中途需要取行李并重新托运。

### 15.4 不需要说复杂英语

现实值机的最佳策略是：

- 工作人员说复杂句；
- 你抓关键词；
- 用短句确认；
- 必要时要求重复；
- 最终把关键事实确认清楚。

这比背长段“机场英语范文”有效得多。

## 16. 练习题

### A. 识别

选择工作人员真正想问什么。

1. **Are you checking in any bags today?**  
   A. 你买行李了吗？  
   B. 你今天有托运行李吗？  
   C. 你找到行李了吗？

2. **Could you put your bag on the scale?**  
   A. 把行李打开  
   B. 把行李放到秤上  
   C. 把行李拿走

3. **Your bag is over the weight limit.**  
   A. 行李超重  
   B. 行李丢失  
   C. 行李超尺寸

4. **Is your bag checked through to Edinburgh?**  
   这里的 `checked through` 最接近：  
   A. 检查过了  
   B. 直挂最终目的地  
   C. 可以带进客舱

**答案：**1-B，2-B，3-A，4-B。

### D. 听力修复

你只听到：

> “... bag ... twenty-three ... limit ...”

你不确定工作人员到底说什么。不要猜，至少说两种可能的修复表达。

参考方向：

- **Sorry, did you say the limit is 23 kilos?**
- **Could you say that again, please?**

### E. 异常处理

情境：

- 你的托运行李 25 kg；
- 免费额度 23 kg；
- 工作人员告诉你要付 £70；
- 你不想付钱；
- 你想问能否拿东西出来重新称重。

要求：不用逐句中文翻译，用 3–5 句把问题解决。

### F. 30 秒口语

不看本文，用 30 秒完成下面任务：

> 你去伦敦，托运一件行李，希望靠过道，最后确认几点登机、哪个登机口。

至少使用 4 个核心句型。

### G. Role switch

第一轮：你扮乘客。  
第二轮：你扮工作人员，并主动加入一个异常：

- 行李超重；或
- 没有靠窗座位；或
- 行李不能直挂。

这样可以训练你真正听懂工作人员的英语，而不只是会背自己的台词。
