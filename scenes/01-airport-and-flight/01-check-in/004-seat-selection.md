## 1. 场景介绍

你在值机柜台或自助机上办手续，系统已经给你分配了一个座位，但你想换：想坐靠窗看风景，想坐过道方便去洗手间，想和同行的人坐在一起，或者想加钱换一个腿部空间更大的位置。

这是一个**你主动提出请求**的场景，和 001、002 有本质区别：那两个场景你是"配合流程"，这里你要**表达偏好、听懂对方给出的选项、然后做出选择**。工作人员通常很愿意帮忙，但座位是有限资源，你会经常听到 "I'm afraid that's all taken" 这类回答，所以本场景的重点不只是"提出要求"，而是**要求被拒绝之后还能问出替代方案**。

英国航司的常见现实是：提前免费选座的窗口很窄，值机时的免费座位往往只剩中间座；extra legroom（加大腿部空间）和 emergency exit row（紧急出口排）几乎都是付费的，而且出口排有额外的资格限制。听懂这些差别，比背几个单词更重要。

## 2. 任务目标

完成本场景后，你应该能：

- 用一句话说清自己想要什么座位（靠窗 / 过道 / 靠前 / 两人相邻）
- 听懂工作人员说的座位类型：aisle / window / middle / extra legroom / exit row / bulkhead
- 听懂"没有了"的几种英文说法，并在被拒绝后继续问替代方案
- 听懂是否要付费，以及大概多少钱
- 听懂紧急出口排的资格要求，并判断自己能不能坐
- 在拿到座位号后复述确认，避免听错行号或字母
- 换座失败时，知道还可以在登机口或上机后再尝试（Scene 018）

## 3. 正常流程

1. 递上护照和订单号，正常开始值机（见 Scene 001）。
2. 工作人员告知系统分配的座位，例如 "You're in 24B."
3. 你提出偏好：`I'd prefer an aisle seat, if possible.`
4. 工作人员查看剩余座位，给出一到两个选项，并说明是否收费。
5. 你确认位置和价格，做出选择：`I'll take the aisle one, thanks.`
6. 工作人员重新打印登机牌，念出新的座位号。
7. 你复述座位号确认，收好登机牌，走向行李托运或安检。

## 4. 常见异常

- 异常 1：你想要的类型已经全部售完，只剩中间座位（middle seat）。
- 异常 2：靠窗座位还有，但只剩在最后一排、靠洗手间的位置。
- 异常 3：想换的座位需要付费，你没料到，需要现场决定要不要花钱。
- 异常 4：你和同行的人被分开了，工作人员只能让你们坐同一排不同段，或前后排。
- 异常 5：你要求紧急出口排，被告知有资格限制（年龄、语言、是否带婴儿），不一定能坐。
- 异常 6：工作人员改好了座位，但登机牌上打印的是旧座位号，需要你自己发现并指出。
- 异常 7：座位在系统里显示可用，但实际是"机组备用座"或损坏座位，最后又被收回。

## 5. 加分场景

- 提出偏好时加上 `if possible`，语气更礼貌，被拒绝时也不尴尬。
- 被拒绝后立刻追问一句 `Is there any aisle seat further back?`，而不是直接放弃。
- 主动问清费用属性：`Is that a one-off charge, or per flight?`
- 换座后主动核对登机牌：`Sorry, could you check the seat number on here? It still says 24B.`
- 如果只想和同伴坐在一起，直接说目标而不是座位类型：`We'd just like to sit together, anywhere is fine.`

## 7. 工作人员最可能说的话

1. **You're in 24B — is that all right?**  
   听力抓手：`24B`、`all right`
2. **Would you prefer a window or an aisle?**  
   听力抓手：`window or aisle`
3. **I'm afraid the aisle seats are all taken.**  
   听力抓手：`all taken`
4. **I've only got middle seats left in that section.**  
   听力抓手：`only`、`middle`、`left`
5. **There's an aisle seat further back, in row thirty-one.**  
   听力抓手：`further back`、`row thirty-one`
6. **Extra legroom is fifteen pounds — would you like it?** *(示例价格：航司按航线和舱位定价，示例非固定)*  
   听力抓手：`extra legroom`、`fifteen pounds`
7. **The exit row seats are chargeable, I'm afraid.**  
   听力抓手：`exit row`、`chargeable`
8. **For the exit row, you need to be over twelve and able to follow instructions in English.**  
   听力抓手：`over sixteen`、`in English`
9. **Are you travelling together?**  
   听力抓手：`travelling together`
10. **I can't put you side by side, but I can do the same row.**  
    听力抓手：`side by side`、`same row`
11. **Would you rather be near the front, or is anywhere fine?**  
    听力抓手：`near the front`、`anywhere fine`
12. **That one's right by the toilets — just so you know.**  
    听力抓手：`by the toilets`
13. **Let me reprint your boarding pass.**  
    听力抓手：`reprint`
14. **You're now in 12C, on the aisle.**  
    听力抓手：`12C`、`on the aisle`
15. **You can also try at the gate if something frees up.**  
    听力抓手：`at the gate`、`frees up`

## 8. 你应该说的话

1. **I'd prefer an aisle seat, if possible.** — 提出偏好，最标准的一句。
2. **I'd prefer a window seat, if possible.** — 换成靠窗。
3. **Is there any aisle seat closer to the front?** — 问更靠前的替代方案。
4. **Is there any window seat further back?** — 问更靠后的替代方案。
5. **Could I have two seats together, please?** — 和同伴一起坐。
6. **We'd just like to sit together — anywhere is fine.** — 直接说目标，不限位置。
7. **Is this the right seat for me? My pass still says 24B.** — 核对登机牌。
8. **How much is the extra legroom seat?** — 问加价座位价格。
9. **Sorry, did you say fifteen pounds?** — 确认金额。
10. **Is there any charge for that one?** — 确认是否收费。
11. **I'll take the aisle one, thanks.** — 完成选择。
12. **I'll take 12C, thank you.** — 用座位号确认选择。
13. **Actually, I'd rather stay where I am.** — 礼貌地放弃换座。
14. **Sorry, is that twelve C, as in Charlie?** — 用字母词确认容易听错的字母。
15. **Could I try again at the gate?** — 问后续机会。

## 10. Patterns

- P028 — I'd prefer + noun, if possible —— 说明：提出偏好但不施压，柜台选座第一句：`I'd prefer an aisle seat, if possible.`
- P029 — Is there any + noun + condition? —— 说明：被拒后追问替代方案，把"没有了"变成"还有别的吗"：`Is there any aisle seat closer to the front?`
- P057 — Is this the right + noun + for me? —— 说明：核对座位号与登机牌是否一致：`Is this the right seat for me?`
- P001 — Could you help me + 动作? —— 说明：请对方帮忙处理同行人分开的情况：`Could you help me sit with my friend?`
- P002 — Could I have + noun, please? —— 说明：直接要两个相邻座位：`Could I have two seats together, please?`
- P016 — Sorry, did you say + 内容? —— 说明：确认价格或座位号：`Sorry, did you say fifteen pounds?`
- P032 — I'll take + noun —— 说明：结束选择，避免反复纠结：`I'll take the aisle one, thanks.`

## 11. Role Play A — Normal

目标：顺利完成任务。

**Agent:** Good morning. Can I have your passport, please?

**You:** Here you are.

**Agent:** Thank you. One bag to check in today?

**You:** Yes, just the one. And I'd prefer an aisle seat, if possible.

**Agent:** Let me have a look. I can put you in 18C — that's an aisle seat, middle of the cabin.

**You:** That sounds good. Is there any charge for that one?

**Agent:** No, that one's free. Here's your boarding pass — 18C, gate information from around eleven twenty.

**You:** Thank you very much.

## 12. Role Play B — Problem

目标：出现一个常见异常后仍完成任务。

**Agent:** Right, you're both on the same booking. You're in 24B and 31E.

**You:** Sorry, did you say twenty-four B and thirty-one E? We're travelling together.

**Agent:** That's right — the system allocated them separately. I'm afraid the aisle seats are all taken.

**You:** We'd just like to sit together — anywhere is fine.

**Agent:** Let me check. I have two seats side by side in row 33, but they're both middle and window.

**You:** That's fine, we'll take those. Is there any charge?

**Agent:** No charge for that. One moment — let me reprint your boarding passes.

**You:** Thanks. Is this the right seat for me? My pass still says 24B.

**Agent:** Ah, that's the old one. Here's the new pair — 33E and 33F.

## 13. Role Play C — Pressure Test

目标：加入真实压力：

- 工作人员说话很快，且把价格和座位号连在一句里
- 后面排队的人一直往前挤，你只有大约 20 秒
- 系统突然显示你原来的座位被机组收回
- 同行人不在身边，你要替两个人做决定
- 广播同时在播其他航班的登机通知

**Agent:** Sorryaboutthat — thatseat'sbeentakenbackbythecrew, soIcanoffer12CorMiddleinrow30, twelve'sfifteenpounds.

**You:** Sorry, could you say that again a little more slowly?

**Agent:** Twelve C is an aisle seat, fifteen pounds. Row 30 middle is free.

**You:** Is that a one-off charge, or per flight?

**Agent:** One-off, this flight only.

**You:** I'll take the aisle one, thanks.

## 16. 易错点

### 易错 1：把 aisle 读成 /eɪsl/

❌ I'd like an /eɪsl/ seat.  
✅ I'd like an aisle seat.（aisle 读 /aɪl/，s 不发音，和 isle 同音）

### 易错 2：用 "change seat" 表示选座

❌ I want to change seat to window.  
✅ I'd prefer a window seat, if possible.（值机时是「选座」，不是「换座」；换座是上机后的事，见 Scene 018）

### 易错 3：直接说 "give me a front seat"

❌ Give me a seat in the front.  
✅ Is there any aisle seat closer to the front?（英国场景里祈使句显得强硬，用问句更自然）

### 易错 4：不确认收费方式就答应

❌ OK, fine.（结果每一段航程都收一次）  
✅ Is that a one-off charge, or per flight?（先问清，再决定）

### 易错 5：拿到新登机牌不核对座位号

❌ 直接走开，登机时才发现打印的还是 24B。  
✅ Sorry, could you check the seat number on here? It still says 24B.

## 17. 扩展说明

费用、限额与流程可能变化，以航空公司官网、机场现场标识或监管机构公告为准。座位费在英国各航空公司差异很大：全服务航司在网上办理登机时通常提供免费选座窗口，低成本航司几乎所有座位都收费，而 extra legroom 与 exit row 一般都是付费座位。

操作习惯上，英国机场柜台的选座空间往往比你想象的小：多数座位在你到达之前已经被线上选座占掉，工作人员看到的只是剩下的零散位置。因此「先说偏好，再接受替代方案」比「反复要求同一种座位」有效得多。如果同行人被分开，明确说出你的真实优先级（`We'd just like to sit together — anywhere is fine.`）通常能更快解决，因为工作人员可以放弃过道/靠窗的限制去找相邻位。登机口还有最后一次机会：`if something frees up` 是常见说法，值得在登机前礼貌问一次。

语言上，这个场景的核心是三步：说偏好（`I'd prefer ... if possible.`）→ 问替代（`Is there any ... closer to the front?`）→ 确认成交（`I'll take the aisle one, thanks.`）。加上一句金额与座位号的复述（`Sorry, did you say fifteen pounds?` / `Sorry, is that twelve C, as in Charlie?`）就足以覆盖绝大多数真实对话。exit row 会被问到年龄与英语能力，如实回答即可，不必解释太多。

## 18. 练习题

**A. 听力理解（判断工作人员的意思）**

1. `I'm afraid the aisle seats are all taken.` —— 工作人员是在（ ）：A 让你补差价　B 告诉你过道座位已满　C 请你去登机口
2. `Extra legroom is fifteen pounds — is that all right?` —— 你需要先确认（ ）：A 金额与是否每段都收　B 行李重量　C 登机口号码
3. `You're both in the same row, 33E and 33F.` —— 这意味着（ ）：A 你们被分开了　B 你们并排　C 需要重新排队
4. `Try at the gate if something frees up.` —— 工作人员建议你（ ）：A 现在付费　B 登机口再问一次有没有空位　C 换航班
