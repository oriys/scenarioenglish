## 1. 场景介绍

你买的是联程票（connecting flight）：先在希思罗飞一段，再到另一个机场或另一个航站楼转下一班。转机时你最需要担心两件事：**行李是不是直挂到最终目的地**，以及**够不够时间、该往哪走**。

行李直挂不是自动的，它取决于**你车票是不是同一个订位**（同一张 booking reference / 一个预订号），而不是只看是不是同一家航空公司。同一订位通常直挂，分订位就往往要你在中转站提取再托运。最稳妥的办法：值机时问一句，落地后看不懂就跟着紫色的 `Flight Connections` 指示走。

顺利结束的样子是：你知道行李被直挂到哪一站、看懂转机指示、重新过完安检、找到下一班的登机口和登机时间。

## 2. 任务目标

完成本场景后，你应该能：

- 用一句话判断并问清行李是否直挂：`Is my bag checked through to my final destination?`
- 听懂工作人员说 `checked through` / `collect and recheck` 的区别并对应行动
- 落地后跟着 `Flight Connections` / `Connections` 指示走，知道要重新过安检
- 问清下一班在哪个航站楼、需要多少时间：`Which terminal is my next flight?` / `How much time do I need?`
- 知道同一订位的转机通常受航司保护、分订位的"自己衔接"不受保护

## 3. 正常流程

1. **值机时（柜台或自助机）确认直挂**：`Is my bag checked through to my final destination?`
2. **看行李挂牌**：挂牌上印的终点城市，就是行李实际会被送到的地方。若只印了第一段机场，说明不直挂到底。
3. 拿好两段登机牌（多数航司会在第一程值机时就把所有航段的登机牌给你），收好行李凭条。
4. 下机后，不看 Arrivals，跟着紫色的 **`Flight Connections`** / **`Connections`** 指示走。
5. **重新过安检**（希思罗所有转机乘客都要再过一次安检）。
6. 大多国际转机**不用再走边检**；但如果是转到英国/爱尔兰境内航班，需要过一次护照查验。
7. 下一班在不同航站楼时，按指示走路（T2↔T3 相通）或坐免费的 Flight Connections 巴士 / 摆渡车。
8. **过完安检后**登机口信息才会显示在屏幕上，到 departure board 查下一班的 gate 和登机时间。
9. 提前至少 20 分钟到登机口（希思罗惯例）。

## 4. 常见异常

- 异常 1：挂牌只印了第一段，说明不直挂到底——需要在中转站提取行李再托运。
- 异常 2：你是**分开订的两个航班**（两张不同的订位号），即使同一家航司也要取行李重托。
- 异常 3：第一班晚点，转机时间不够。同一订位通常航司会免费改签；分开订位则没这个保护。
- 异常 4：下一班在另一个航站楼，且那个转机需要过边检/入境（有些机场隔了国境）。
- 异常 5：你没拿到第二段登机牌，需要去 transfer / connections desk 补打。
- 异常 6：广播/屏幕找不到第二班的登机口（有时登机口会在临近才显示）。
- 异常 7：去美国或中国的首入国航班，即使同一订位也可能要先取行李（当地法规）。

## 5. 加分场景

- 分流前主动问 `Do I need to collect and recheck my bag?` 而不是自己猜。
- 转机时间紧张时，问 `Will I have enough time to make it?` 并向工作人员求助。
- 听懂转机广播 `passengers for flight XXX, please proceed to gate Y` 并对照行动。
- 发现第二段缺登机牌，主动找 `transfer desk` 补打而不是干等。

## 8. 你应该说的话

1. **Is my bag checked through to my final destination?** — 值机时确认直挂。
2. **Do I need to collect and recheck it in [hub]?** — 不确定时问清要不要取行李。
3. **Which terminal is my next flight leaving from?** — 问下一班起飞的航站楼。
4. **How much time do I need to make the connection?** — 问需要多少转机时间。
5. **Will I have enough time to make it?** — 时间紧时问来得及吗。
6. **Where do I go after I land — Connections or Baggage Reclaim?** — 下机后问走哪条路。
7. **I don't have my onward boarding pass. Where's the transfer desk?** — 问去哪里补登机牌。
8. **Is this a separate booking — do I need to recheck my bag?** — 主动确认分订位。
9. **Sorry, could you say that again? You'll need to...?** — 没听清时复述确认。
10. **Do I need to go through passport control on this connection?** — 问要不要过边检。
11. **My first flight was delayed — am I protected for the next one?** — 问晚点转机保护。
12. **What time is boarding for the next flight?** — 确认下一班登机时间。

## 10. Patterns

### New Patterns

- P062 — Is + obj + checked through to + 地点? —— 问行李是否直挂终点：`Is my bag checked through to Paris?`

### Review Patterns

- P001, P006 — 基础确认 / 没听清复述（`Sorry, could you say that again?`）
- P049 — Will I have enough time + V? —— 问时间是否够：`Will I have enough time to make it?`
- P050 — Am I allowed to + V? —— 确认是否允许/必要：`Do I need to collect and recheck it?`
- P055 — Which gate/terminal + V? —— 问登机口/航站楼：`Which terminal is my next flight from?`
- P058 — Could you show me where + 名词 + is? —— 问位置：`Where's the transfer desk?`

## 11. Role Play A — Normal

目标：顺利确认直挂并完成转机。

**You:** Hi, I have a connection, just checking — is my bag checked through to my final destination?

**Agent:** Yes, your bags are checked through to Toronto. All on the one booking, is that right?

**You:** Yes, one booking. Great, thank you.

**Agent:** Keep your bag tags and your second boarding pass. Follow the Flight Connections signs after you land.

**You:** Do I need to go through security again?

**Agent:** Yes, everyone does at Heathrow. Liquids can stay in your bag now.

**You:** And where do I find my next gate?

**Agent:** The screens after security will show your gate. You'll have time — the connection is about three hours.

**You:** Perfect. Thank you very much.

## 12. Role Play B — Problem

目标：发现行李需要重托，仍把转机办成。

**You:** Hi, I'm connecting here. Can you check if my bag goes all the way through?

**Agent:** Let me look. Actually, this is a separate booking, so you'll need to collect your bag and recheck it.

**You:** Okay — do I need to collect and recheck it, then?

**Agent:** Yes. Exit through Arrivals, get your bag, then check in at Departures for your next flight.

**You:** Will I have enough time to do all that? My next flight's in two hours.

**Agent:** It's tight. I'd go straight to Baggage Reclaim now, then hurry back.

**You:** Sorry, could you say that again — I go to Arrivals, collect, and back to Departures, is that right?

**Agent:** Exactly. Then re-clear security and go to the gate.

**You:** Got it. Which terminal is my next flight from?

**Agent:** Terminal 3 — from here it's an easy walk.

**You:** Thanks, I'll move fast.

## 13. Role Play C — Pressure Test

目标：转机时间紧、信息不清时仍不漏关键动作。

**You:** Excuse me, my connection's in 40 minutes. Am I in time?

**Agent:** Probably — gate's on the board now. You're Terminal 5.

**You:** Will I have enough time to make it?

**Agent:** Take the free Flight Connections bus, it runs every few minutes.

**You:** Do I need to collect my bag, or is it through?

**Agent:** It's checked through to your final destination. You don't need to claim it.

**You:** I'm not sure I heard that — is my bag checked through, yes or no?

**Agent:** Yes, it's checked through. No need to collect.

**You:** Great. And I don't need to go through immigration?

**Agent:** Not for an international transfer like this one. Security, then your gate.

**You:** Where's the bus for Terminal 5?

**Agent:** Follow the Flight Connections signs to your left, you can't miss it.

**You:** Thanks — sorry, one more, what time does boarding close?

**Agent:** Twenty minutes before departure. Head there now.

**You:** Will do. Thank you so much.

## 16. 易错点

### 易错 1：以为同一家航司就一定直挂

❌ 以为都是 BA 的航班行李就自动直挂。  
✅ 判断标准是**同一个订位号**。分开订的两段，即使同一家航司，也要中途取行李再托运。值机时用 `Is my bag checked through...?` 确认。

### 易错 2：以为"转机永远不取行李"

❌ 把 `you never collect on a connection` 当铁律。  
✅ 分开订位、以及去美国/中国等首入国航段，可能必须取行李。落地后跟着 `Connections` 走，需要时再处理。

### 易错 3：混淆"够不够时间"和"受不受保护"

❌ 觉得分开订、时间留得长就安全。  
✅ 同一订位（受保护）：第一班晚点航司通常免费改签；分开订（自衔接）：没保护，第一班晚点你就可能赶不上而且没人负责。两个是不同概念。

### 易错 4：下了机跟着 Arrivals 走

❌ 转机时跟着 `Arrivals / Baggage Reclaim` 走，去了取行李的地方。  
✅ 转机要跟 `Flight Connections / Connections` 走；`Arrivals` 是你需要取行李时才去的方向。

### 易错 5：在安检前找登机口

❌ 一下机就急着找第二班的 gate 号码。  
✅ 希思罗的登机口信息**过完安检后**才上屏。先过安检，再看 departure board。

## 17. 扩展说明

**订位与直挂**：行李直挂主要看是否同一订位（同一 booking reference），而非是否同一家航司。同一订位的 oneworld/联盟航班即使票号不同通常也直挂；分开订位每段是独立行程。此为国际联运通行做法，但不同航司/联盟可能有细微差异——拿不准就值机时问。

**转机时间与保护**：同一订位有航司给的最短转机时间（MCT），早班晚点会保护你（免费改签）；分开订位没有 MCT 也没有保护，需要自己留足时间（希思罗建议分开票预留 4 小时以上）。具体数值因机场/航司而异，别背死。

**边检与签证**：纯国际空侧转机通常不用再过边检；但如果转英国/爱尔兰境内航班、或在某些机场跨航站楼转机会进入国境，就要过护照查验。需要不需要申根过境签证因人/线路而异——不确定就问 `Do I need to go through passport control on this connection?`。

**航站楼间转机**：希思罗 T2↔T3 步行可达，T3↔T5 有免费 Flight Connections 巴士（每 6–10 分钟一班），T4 可坐摆渡或希思罗快线。具体以机场现场指引为准。

**_last_verified: 2026-08_。直挂规则、转机时间、边检/签证要求都会随运营商、机场、路线和季节变化，正式出行前以航空公司与机场官网为准。**
