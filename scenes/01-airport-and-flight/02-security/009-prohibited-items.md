## 1. 场景介绍

有时候安检会发现你包里有一件**不能带上机**的东西：超量的液体、一把超过 6cm 的刀、一根棒球棍、一瓶打火机油、或者第二根充电宝。工作人员会告诉你："**This isn't allowed in hand baggage.**"

这时候你**不用慌，也不是犯法**——这是安检的常规处置。你通常有几个选项：**能托运的拿去托运**、**当场放弃**、或**少数机场帮你寄回/暂存**。要做的就是把话听懂、问清选项、做个决定、礼貌配合。

**注意：不是所有东西都能托运。** 一把刀可以托运，但打火机油、易燃品、多出的充电宝连托运也不行。听到 `It's not allowed in hold either` 就说明只能放弃。

顺利结束的样子是：你弄清这件东西的选项、做出了决定（托运/放弃/寄回），然后继续去登机口，没被拦下一整程。

## 2. 任务目标

完成本场景后，你应该能：

- 认出并说出常见被拦的物品种类（超量液体、锋利物品、体育器械、危险品、充电宝）
- 听懂并回应"这件不能带上机"（`This isn't allowed in hand baggage`）
- 问清处置选项：`Can I put it in my checked luggage?` / `What are my options?`
- 明白"托运"不是万能——危险品、充电宝连托运也不行
- 平静做出决定（托运 / 放弃 / 寄回）并用短英语表达
- 知道免税密封袋不可拆、以及需要时回值机柜台托运

## 3. 正常流程

1. 安检员指着一件物品说 "This isn't allowed in hand baggage"，并说明它是什么。
2. 你确认听懂，平静回应 `Oh, okay. What are my options?`
3. 对方给出选项（可能是一到两个）：
   - 能托运的 → `You can put it in your checked luggage.`
   - 不能托运的 → `It's not allowed in hold either.`
   - 有的机场 → `We can post it back / you can collect it later.`
4. 你选一个并表达清楚你的决定。
5. 若选托运，跟指引回到值机柜台办理（可能要走回陆侧再重新过安检）。
6. 若选放弃，把东西放进处置箱 / 交给对方。
7. 拿齐其余物品，继续去登机口。

## 4. 常见异常

- 异常 1：超量液体被拦（最常见）。有时可以回值机柜台托运，有时只能放弃。
- 异常 2：带了一把超过 6cm 的刀——可以托运，但你要走回值机柜台。
- 异常 3：带了大瓶打火机油——连托运都不行，只能放弃。
- 异常 4：免税买的酒，密封袋被拆开了——可能被扣下。
- 异常 5：不清楚"托运"已关闭（值机结束）——需要问是否还来得及。
- 异常 6：东西多、值机早就托了——回程托运还开不开，问清楚。

## 5. 加分场景

- 主动问 `Is it allowed in hold?`，显得懂得处理。
- 听到 item 名能对上号：knife、scissors、bottle、power bank。
- 平静选择 `OK, you can take it, then.` 并继续办自己的事。
- 发现是免税袋，主动问 `Should I keep it sealed?`

## 7. 工作人员最可能说的话

1. **I'm sorry, but this knife isn't allowed in hand baggage.**  
   听力抓手：`isn't allowed`
2. **This bottle is over 100ml, so it can't go through security.**  
   听力抓手：`over 100ml`
3. **You have three choices — I can take it, you can check it in, or we can send it home.**  
   听力抓手：`three choices`
4. **Would you like to check it in, or leave it with us?**  
   听力抓手：`check it in ... leave it`
5. **If you want to check it in, you'll need to go back to your airline's desk.**  
   听力抓手：`go back to your airline's desk`
6. **It's not allowed in hold baggage either, I'm afraid.**  
   听力抓手：`not allowed ... either`
7. **Keep the duty-free bag sealed, please.**  
   听力抓手：`keep ... sealed`

## 8. 你应该说的话

1. **Oh, I see. What are my options?** — 问处置选项。
2. **Can I put it in my checked luggage instead?** — 问能不能托运。
3. **Is it allowed in hold?** — 直接问能否托运。
4. **Can I go back and check it in? Is there time?** — 问回柜台托运是否来得及。
5. **Okay, you can take it, then. / Fine, I'll throw it away.** — 决定放弃。
6. **Could you send it back to me instead?** — 问能否寄回（如果有这服务）。
7. **What if I keep it sealed?** — 免税袋被打过要再确认。
8. **Sorry, could you say that again — check it in or leave it?** — 复述确认选项。

## 11. Role Play A — Normal

目标：超量液体被拦，平稳处理。

**Officer:** I'm sorry, but this bottle is more than 100ml — it can't go through security.

**You:** Oh, I see. What are my options?

**Officer:** You can leave it with me, or if you prefer, you can go back and check it in.

**You:** Can I put it in my checked luggage? Is there time?

**Officer:** Your airline's desk is just to your left, it should be fine.

**You:** Okay, I'd like to check it in, then. Where exactly do I go?

**Officer:** Head back through that door to check-in, desk 14.

**You:** Great, thank you. I'll be quick.

**Officer:** No problem. Come back through security after.

**You:** Will do. Thanks so much.

## 12. Role Play B — Problem

目标：锋利物品+无法托运的情况，做决定。

**Officer:** Sir, this is a pocket knife — it can't go in hand baggage.

**You:** Oh, really? It's just a small multitool.

**Officer:** The blade is over 6cm, so it's not allowed in the cabin. It can go in hold, though.

**You:** Is there time to check it in? My flight's in an hour.

**Officer:** Should be, yes. Back to the check-in desk, put it in your hold bag.

**You:** What if I just leave it with you instead?

**Officer:** That's fine too — you can surrender it.

**You:** Actually, I'd rather keep it. Can it go in my checked baggage?

**Officer:** Yes. Go to your airline's desk now, before the hold closes.

**You:** Okay, I'll do that. Can you tell me which way?

**Officer:** Through to Departures, then desk 6. Hurry a little.

**You:** Will do, thanks for letting me know.

## 13. Role Play C — Pressure Test

目标：危险品+时间紧张+选项不清，仍做对决定。

**Officer:** Ma'am, this lighter fuel can't be taken, I'm afraid.

**You:** Sorry, can't it go in my checked luggage?

**Officer:** No, it's not allowed in hold either. Flammable items can't fly in any baggage.

**You:** I see. So what are my options?

**Officer:** You can leave it with us — that's the only option here.

**You:** Okay, you can take it, then. That's fine.

**Officer:** Thank you. And this power bank — is it the only one you have?

**You:** Yes, just one, it's in my hand luggage.

**Officer:** That's fine, keep it on you. It can't be checked in.

**You:** Right, got it. So I surrender the lighter fuel and keep the power bank?

**Officer:** Exactly. You're all set.

**You:** Great, thanks for your help. Sorry about the fuel.

**Officer:** No problem. Enjoy your flight.

## 16. 易错点

### 易错 1：以为所有东西都能托运

❌ 被拦了就想"那托运呗"。  
✅ 刀、剪刀能托运；但打火机油、易燃品、腐蚀品、烟花、多出的充电宝**连托运都不行**。听到 `not allowed in hold either` 就只剩放弃/寄回。

### 易错 2：对"加起来"而不是"看懂"较劲

❌ 觉得被没收很冤，反复争辩。  
✅ 争取没有太大帮助，物件就是不能上机。平静了解选项、做决定、继续行程，最省事。

### 易错 3：不知道液体是最常见被拦项

❌ 以为"放包里的水没人管"。  
✅ 超量液体是安检最常见的没收对象。能托运的就去托运，不行就放弃，别抱侥幸。

### 易错 4：免税袋被拆开还硬装

❌ 在安检后区买的酒，透明密封袋(STEB)被打开过，还想带过下一程。  
✅ 免税液体要**保持密封袋完整、收据可见**；拆开就可能被扣，尤其返程/转机。

### 易错 5：不清楚托运已经/还没关闭

❌ 想托运但值机已经关闭，白跑一趟。  
✅ 回柜台前问一句 `Is there time to check it in?`，别自作主张。

## 17. 扩展说明

**6cm 规则**：刀刃超过 6cm 严禁进客舱、可托运（大剪刀同理）；≤6cm 的小剪刀、圆头剪刀、指甲刀、镊子、开瓶器（简单型）可以带上机。这条相对稳定。

**禁带 vs 限制带**：锋利尖锐超过 6cm、棒球/板球/高尔夫球杆等体育器械进客舱禁止、托运需航司确认；打火机油、易燃气体、腐蚀品、烟花、非安全火柴、湿式汽车电瓶等**客舱托运都禁**；充电宝/备用锂电池只能随身、防短路、每人最多 2 个、≤100Wh 无需审批（100–160Wh 需航司）、严禁托运。

**选项确实存在**：很多机场和第三方公司（如 Excess Baggage）提供把禁带物品**寄回或限期（约 30 天）自取**的有偿服务——但不是每家机场都有。对方会告诉你有没有，`What are my options?` 就是为这个准备的。

**别把它当法律问题**：没收是航空安全运营决定，不是刑事案。平静配合、语言客气，大家都不难受。

**_last_verified: 2026-08_。锋利 6cm、充电宝随身、危险品全禁是相对稳定的规则；液体上限逐机场变化；"寄回/自取"服务和费用因机场而异，以现场说法为准。**

## 18. 练习题

### A. 识别

1. 听到 `This knife isn't allowed in hand baggage`——它一般能托运吗？
2. `It's not allowed in hold either` 意味着你还有什么选项？
3. 下列哪项**不能**托运？（刀 / 大剪刀 / 打火机油 / 高尔夫球杆）

### B. Pattern 替换

1. 用 P004 问：能不能托运这样东西。
2. 用 P065 问：还有什么选项 / 能不能帮忙。
3. 用 P051 问：液体限制是多少。
4. 用 P003 请求：请指一下值机柜台在哪。

### D. 异常处理

情境：你带了把大剪刀，安检说是禁带品，但你的航班 45 分钟后值机可能已关闭。要求：不用逐句翻译，用英文问清能否托运、来得及吗，并做出决定。
