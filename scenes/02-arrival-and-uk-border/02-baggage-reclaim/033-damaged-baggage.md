## 1. 场景介绍

拿到箱子发现轮子掉了、拉链坏了、箱体裂了。本场景训练：**在机场当场报告损坏、拍照留证、报 PIR、了解索赔时限**。CAA 明确：损坏要**到达机场当场报**，书面索赔 **7 天内**。

任务起点：你取到行李时发现损坏。
成功结果：你拍照、报 PIR、拿到副本、清楚下一步。

## 2. 任务目标

完成本场景后，你应该能：

- 当场发现并检查损坏（轮子/拉链/把手/箱体）
- 拍照留证（损坏部位+行李牌+整体）
- 找航司 Baggage Services 报 PIR
- 描述损坏情况
- 了解修/换/赔三种可能和 7 天时限

## 3. 正常流程

1. **当场发现**：取到行李时检查轮子、拉链、把手、箱体。
2. **拍照留证**：损坏部位特写 + 行李牌 + 箱子整体。
3. 去航司 `Baggage Services` 柜台，出示登机牌+行李牌。
4. 报 PIR：描述损坏（`The wheel is broken / the zip is torn`），提供照片。
5. 拿 PIR 副本。
6. 后续：航司可能修、换箱或赔钱；书面索赔 **7 天内**提交（照片+价值证明）。

## 4. 常见异常

- 异常 1：离开机场后才报，难以认定责任。
- 异常 2：没拍照，口说无凭。
- 异常 3：描述不清损坏部位。
- 异常 4：不知道 7 天时限，错过索赔。
- 异常 5：把正常磨损当损坏（划痕一般不赔）。

## 5. 加分场景

- 取行李时当场检查（不急着走）。
- 拍照时连同行李牌一起拍（证明是这个箱子）。
- 主动问：`Will you repair it or replace it?`

## 11. Role Play A — Normal

目标：当场报告损坏、报 PIR。

**You:** Excuse me, my suitcase is damaged. The wheel is broken.

**Staff:** I'm sorry to hear that. Can you show me?

**You:** Here — this wheel came off, and the corner is cracked.

**Staff:** Do you have a photo?

**You:** Yes, I took one before I left the carousel.

**Staff:** Perfect. Was it like this when you collected it?

**You:** Yes, exactly. I checked it right at the carousel.

**Staff:** We'll note it on a PIR. Here's your copy.

**You:** Will you repair it or replace it?

**Staff:** We'll assess it — either repair or replace, and you can also claim in writing within 7 days.

**You:** Understood. What do I need to send?

**Staff:** The PIR number, photos, and any receipts for the bag.

**You:** Thank you very much.

## 12. Role Play B — Problem

目标：不确定是否值得报 + 需要知道怎么索赔。

**You:** Excuse me, is this damage worth reporting? The zip is torn but the bag still closes.

**Staff:** It's up to you — if it affects use, report it. We can note it on a PIR.

**You:** I think it does affect it. The zip keeps catching.

**Staff:** Fine, let's do the PIR. Your boarding pass, please.

**You:** Here. And what's the process after this?

**Staff:** Send your written claim with the PIR number within 7 days, plus a photo.

**You:** And will I be compensated for a new bag?

**Staff:** It depends on the assessment and the bag's value — we don't do new-for-old.

**You:** I understand. Thanks for explaining.

## 13. Role Play C — Pressure Test

目标：转机赶时间 + 需要快速处理。

**You:** Excuse me, I have a tight connection and my bag came out damaged.

**Staff:** Let's be quick then. Where's the damage?

**You:** The handle is snapped — I can't pull the bag.

**Staff:** Can I see a photo?

**You:** Here, I took it right away.

**Staff:** Great. Here's your PIR — that's all we need now.

**You:** And the claim?

**Staff:** Write to us within 7 days with the PIR number. Keep it simple.

**You:** Will I be contacted, or do I contact you?

**Staff:** We'll assess and contact you. Check your email.

**You:** Okay, thanks for making it fast.

**Staff:** Safe travels — go catch your flight.

## 16. 易错点

### 易错 1：离开机场才报

损坏**当场报**最有利——离场后再报很难证明责任。

### 易错 2：不拍照

先拍照再报。损坏部位 + 行李牌 + 整体三张图。

### 易错 3：描述不清

用具体词：`wheel / zip / handle / cracked / torn / snapped`。

### 易错 4：错过 7 天时限

书面索赔 **7 天内**（损坏/丢失）。拿到 PIR 后立刻准备材料。

### 易错 5：把磨损当损坏

正常使用划痕（wear and tear）一般不赔——别为小划痕浪费时间。

## 17. 扩展说明

### 17.1 三种处理结果

航司可能：**维修**（repair）、**换箱**（replace）、**赔钱**（compensate）——取决于损坏程度和箱子价值评估。

### 17.2 折旧原则

赔偿按**折旧价值**计算，"新换旧"不算。所以保留购买凭证（receipt）很重要。

### 17.3 为什么当场检查

CAA 建议取行李时当场检查——损坏报告要在机场完成，PIR 是索赔基础。
