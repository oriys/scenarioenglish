> **Path:** 02 入境英国 → 04 抵达后的第一小时 → 买 SIM/eSIM、连接 Wi-Fi

## 1. 场景介绍

落地第一件事：**连上网**。本场景训练：连机场免费 Wi-Fi、买本地 SIM/eSIM、处理手机网络问题。没有网寸步难行——这是"抵达第一小时"的第一个场景。

任务起点：你刚出关，手机还没网。
成功结果：你顺利连上 Wi-Fi 或买到 SIM/eSIM，能上网了。

## 2. 任务目标

完成本场景后，你应该能：

- 连机场免费 Wi-Fi（看懂验证流程）
- 买本地 SIM/eSIM（选套餐、出示护照）
- 说清需求：只要流量/要打电话/要 eSIM
- 网络出问题时求助

## 3. 正常流程

1. 落地开机 → 找机场免费 Wi-Fi（如 `Heathrow Wi-Fi`）。
2. 连接 → 浏览器弹出页面 → `Get online` → 填邮箱 → 确认。
3. 需要本地号码/流量 → 找运营商柜台（EE/Vodafone/O2/Three）或便利店。
4. 说明需求：`I'd like a prepaid SIM with data, please.`
5. 选套餐：`What data plans do you have?`
6. 出示护照（实名要求）→ 付款 → 激活。
7. 确认能上网后继续行程。

## 4. 常见异常

- 异常 1：找不到 Wi-Fi 验证页面/看不懂表单词汇。
- 异常 2：不会说 prepaid / pay-as-you-go。
- 异常 3：不知道有没有 eSIM，买了实体卡却发现手机不支持。
- 异常 4：激活失败，店员说的激活步骤没听懂。
- 异常 5：Wi-Fi 要短信验证码但手机没信号。

## 5. 加分场景

- 出发前确认手机支持 eSIM（行前买好，落地即用）。
- 主动说：`I just need data — I don't need calls.`
- 让店员确认：`Can I use this in my phone? / Is it eSIM compatible?`

## 11. Role Play A — Normal

目标：买 SIM 卡全流程。

**You:** Hi, I'd like a prepaid SIM card, please.

**Staff:** Sure. Do you need calls or just data?

**You:** Just data — I don't need calls.

**Staff:** This one is £10 for 20GB for 30 days. That works for most visitors.

**You:** That sounds good. Can I use it in my phone?

**Staff:** Yes, it's compatible with most phones. Is your phone unlocked?

**You:** Yes, it is. Do I need my passport?

**Staff:** Yes, can I see it, please? ... Thanks. It'll be active in about 10 minutes.

**You:** Great. Can you help me activate it?

**Staff:** I'll insert it now — done. You'll see the signal shortly.

**You:** Perfect, thank you very much.

## 12. Role Play B — Problem

目标：Wi-Fi 连不上 + 手机设置问题。

**You:** Excuse me, I can't connect to the Wi-Fi. Can you help?

**Staff:** Sure — which network are you trying?

**You:** The one that says free airport Wi-Fi. It asks for an email.

**Staff:** Yes, just enter your email and confirm — you'll get a code.

**You:** I did, but it says "connection failed". And I have no signal for the code.

**Staff:** That's the issue — the code comes by SMS. Let me check...

**You:** Is there a code-free way?

**Staff:** Some networks have a guest option — try 'Airport-Guest' without a password.

**You:** Let me try that... It works! Thank you so much.

**Staff:** No problem. Enjoy your stay.

## 13. Role Play C — Pressure Test

目标：赶时间 + 需要立刻联网。

**You:** Hi, I'm in a hurry — I need a SIM right now. Just data, nothing else.

**Staff:** Quick options: this £10 for 20GB, or the eSIM one you scan instantly.

**You:** eSIM — my phone supports it. No passport needed?

**Staff:** For eSIM, we still need ID for registration. Can you scan your passport here?

**You:** Sure. (扫描) Done. How fast is activation?

**Staff:** Usually instant — you'll get a notification within a minute.

**You:** And if it doesn't work?

**Staff:** Come back or call this number — we're open 24 hours.

**You:** Great. Thanks for being quick.

**Staff:** You're welcome — safe travels.

## 15. 英国本地表达

- 英式 `pay-as-you-go`（PAYG）= 预付费（充值即用）。
- `top up` = 充值，手机店常问 `Do you need a top up?`
- `sorted` 又出现了——`all sorted` = 都办好了。
- 英式说 `mobile` 或 `phone`，很少说 cellphone。

## 16. 易错点

### 易错 1：说不清"只要流量"

`I just need data` 一句话解决——别让店员推荐包含通话的贵套餐。

### 易错 2：不知道 prepaid 怎么说

`prepaid SIM` / `pay-as-you-go` 是核心词。不会就说 `a SIM card, please`。

### 易错 3：手机不支持 eSIM 却买 eSIM

买前确认：`Is it eSIM compatible?` 或 `Does my phone support eSIM?`

### 易错 4：Wi-Fi 验证看不懂

表单词：`email`（邮箱）、`confirm`（确认）、`connect`（连接）。需要短信验证码时说明没信号。

### 易错 5：不问激活时间

`How long until it's active?` 问一句——有些卡要等 10 分钟。

## 17. 扩展说明

### 17.1 eSIM vs 实体卡

2026 年 eSIM 普及度高：手机支持的话扫码即装，**行前买好落地即用**是最省事的方案。实体卡需要找柜台+实名。

### 17.2 机场 Wi-Fi 通常免费

大多数英国机场提供免费 Wi-Fi，一般要邮箱验证。先连 Wi-Fi 再买卡，能省流量费。

### 17.3 实名要求

英国买预付费 SIM 需要身份验证（护照）——不是麻烦，是法规。带上护照去买。

### D. 异常处理

情境：你手机支持 eSIM 但店员给了实体卡，且你赶时间。要求：用英文说明需求（eSIM 即时激活）、询问能否换成 eSIM、确认激活时间。

### E. 迁移练习

把"说明需求+确认兼容"用到租 Wi-Fi 蛋、买充电宝、租手机时——先确认兼容再付款。
