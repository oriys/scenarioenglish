> **Path:** 02 入境英国 → 04 抵达后的第一小时 → 取现、换汇与银行卡问题

## 1. 场景介绍

落地后需要现金或处理银行卡问题。本场景训练：**ATM 取现、换汇柜台、刷卡/拒付问题**。钱的事说清楚很重要。

任务起点：你需要现金，或刷卡遇到问题。
成功结果：你顺利取现/换汇，或用卡支付没有障碍。

## 2. 任务目标

完成本场景后，你应该能：

- 看懂 ATM 操作提示并取现
- 在换汇柜台说明需求、问清费率
- 处理卡被拒、现金不足等支付问题
- 用 contactless（闪付）支付

## 3. 正常流程

1. 需要现金 → 找 ATM（到达厅有）或换汇柜台（Currency Exchange / Bureau de change）。
2. ATM：`Insert your card` → `Enter PIN` → `Select amount` → `Take your cash`。
3. 换汇：`I'd like to exchange £200 worth of cash, please.` → 出示护照 → 问费率手续费。
4. 刷卡：小额常用 contactless；`Do you take contactless?` / `Can I pay by card?`
5. 卡被拒：`My card was declined — can you try again?` → 联系银行。

## 4. 常见异常

- 异常 1：换汇不问费率/手续费。
- 异常 2：ATM 的货币选择（DCC）看不懂。
- 异常 3：卡被拒慌张，不会求助。
- 异常 4：没现金也不会说刷卡。
- 异常 5：ATM 吞卡/机器故障。

## 5. 加分场景

- ATM 提示选货币时选**英镑结算**（避开不利汇率）。
- 提前跟银行开通境外取现/消费。
- 主动问：`Is there a fee for using this ATM?`

## 8. 你应该说的话

1. **Where's the nearest ATM?** — 找 ATM。
2. **I'd like to take out £100, please.** — 取现。
3. **Can I exchange money here?** — 换汇。
4. **What's your exchange rate? / Is there a fee?** — 问费率。
5. **My card isn't working — can you help?** — 卡问题。
6. **Do you accept contactless?** — 问闪付。
7. **I don't have cash — can I pay by card?** — 刷卡。
8. **Can I pay in pounds, please?** — 选币种。

## 11. Role Play A — Normal

目标：ATM 取现顺利。

**You:** Excuse me, where's the nearest ATM?

**Staff:** There's one just past the currency exchange desk.

**You:** Thanks. (到 ATM，屏幕上显示: "Insert your card and enter your PIN") 

**You:** (操作) It's asking which currency I want to pay in.

**Staff:** Choose pounds — it's better for you usually.

**You:** Okay, I'll choose pounds. (取出现金) 

**Staff:** All good?

**You:** Yes, got my cash. Thank you!

## 12. Role Play B — Problem

目标：换汇问清费率 + 卡被拒。

**You:** Hi, can I exchange money here?

**Staff:** Yes, of course. What currency do you have?

**You:** Euros. I'd like to exchange about €200.

**Staff:** The rate today is 1.15 — so you'd get about £173.

**You:** Is there a commission?

**Staff:** No commission, but there's a small fee of £3.

**You:** That's fine. Do I need my passport?

**Staff:** Yes, please. And here's your money and receipt.

**You:** Thank you. Actually — my card was declined at the shop earlier. Can you check if there's an issue?

**Staff:** That could be the foreign transaction block. Call your bank — the number on your card.

**You:** I'll do that. Thanks for the help.

## 13. Role Play C — Pressure Test

目标：没现金 + 卡又出问题，快速解决。

**You:** Hi, I don't have cash and my card was just declined. Can you help?

**Staff:** Let's sort this out. Which card did you try?

**You:** My credit card. It worked in China but not here.

**Staff:** It might need a foreign transaction approval. Do you have another card?

**You:** Yes, my debit card — let me try it. (试) It works!

**Staff:** Great. Also — has your bank been notified you're travelling?

**You:** No, I didn't think to do that.

**Staff:** Call them and let them know — that prevents these blocks.

**You:** I'll call now. Thanks for saving me!

**Staff:** No problem. Enjoy your trip.

## 14. 听不懂时的万能应对句

- **Sorry, could you say that again?** — 请求重复。
- **What's the fee for this?** — 问费用。
- **Did you say £3 or £13?** — 数字确认。
- **Could you help me with this machine?** — 求助机器。
- **Why was my card declined?** — 问原因。

## 15. 英国本地表达

- 英式 `cash machine` = ATM；`contactless` 就是闪付。
- `pounds` = 英镑（£），口语常说 `quid`（£20 = twenty quid）。
- 店员说 `all good?` = 都好了吗。
- `sorted` 又来了——`we've sorted it` = 解决了。

## 16. 易错点

### 易错 1：换汇不问费率手续费

先问 `What's the exchange rate? Is there a fee?` 再换。

### 易错 2：ATM 选错结算货币

ATM 问用英镑还是本国货币结算——**选英镑**通常更划算（避免 DCC 汇率陷阱）。

### 易错 3：卡被拒就慌

先问 `Can you try again?` 换张卡试，再联系银行。别在柜台前干着急。

### 易错 4：没现金还不说刷卡

`Can I pay by card?` 一句解决——英国普遍支持刷卡。

### 易错 5：没提前通知银行

出境前给银行报备（travel notification）能避免大部分境外拒付。

## 17. 扩展说明

### 17.1 英国刷卡环境

英国基本无现金化程度高，小额也常用 contactless 闪付。备少量现金应急即可。

### 17.2 汇率怎么选

机场换汇通常费率一般（图方便）；ATM 取现按卡组织汇率。**核心：问清费用再操作**。

### 17.3 拒付常见原因

境外交易未开通、余额不足、风控拦截。联系银行客服（卡背面电话）通常即时解决。
