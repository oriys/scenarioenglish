> **Path:** 02 入境英国 → 03 海关 → 被海关询问

## 1. 场景介绍

走红色通道或随机抽查时，海关官员会开箱/询问。本场景训练：**配合检查、回答关于行李内容的问题、被要求开箱时怎么做**。

任务起点：海关官员叫住你/要求检查。
成功结果：你配合完成检查，顺利通过。

## 2. 任务目标

完成本场景后，你应该能：

- 回答海关常见问题（从哪来/行李里有什么/是否申报）
- 配合开箱检查
- 描述行李里的物品（clothes/gifts/medication/food）
- 听不懂时主动修复
- 面对补税/没收时冷静应对

## 3. 正常流程

1. 海关官员叫住你：`Could you come here, please?` / `Random check.`
2. 常见问题：`Where have you come from?` `What's in your bag?` `Do you have anything to declare?`
3. 要求开箱：`Could you open your bag, please?` → 配合打开。
4. 官员查看，可能问物品：`What's this?`
5. 发现未申报超额：官员说明（补税/没收），配合处理。
6. 正常通过：`That's fine, you can go.`

## 4. 常见异常

- 异常 1：慌张抗拒，态度僵硬。
- 异常 2：听不懂问题却乱答。
- 异常 3：解释不清物品（这是什么/干什么用）。
- 异常 4：不知道后果，不敢问。
- 异常 5：开箱时手忙脚乱。

## 5. 加分场景

- 主动配合：`Sure, no problem.`
- 听不懂直接问：`Sorry, could you say that again?`
- 描述清晰：`It's my personal medication with a prescription.`

## 11. Role Play A — Normal

目标：配合抽查顺利通过。

**Staff:** Could you come here, please? This is a random check.

**You:** Sure, no problem.

**Staff:** Could you open your bag, please?

**You:** Of course — here you go.

**Staff:** Thanks. What's in the suitcase?

**You:** Clothes, gifts for my family, and some books.

**Staff:** Anything to declare?

**You:** No, nothing over my allowance.

**Staff:** That's fine. You can go. Enjoy your stay.

**You:** Thank you very much.

## 12. Role Play B — Problem

目标：被问物品 + 有点紧张。

**Staff:** What's in this plastic bag?

**You:** It's... some food from home. Snacks.

**Staff:** What kind of snacks?

**You:** Some dried meat and biscuits. Is that a problem?

**Staff:** The dried meat isn't allowed from outside the EU, I'm afraid.

**You:** Oh no, I'm sorry. I didn't realise. My mum packed it for me.

**Staff:** I understand. We'll have to confiscate it — but the biscuits are fine.

**You:** Okay, that's fair. Can I keep the biscuits?

**Staff:** Yes, of course. Anything else?

**You:** No, that's everything. Thank you for explaining.

## 13. Role Play C — Pressure Test

目标：超额度被要求补税 + 保持镇定。

**Staff:** How many cigarettes are you carrying?

**You:** About 300 — I bought them at the duty-free.

**Staff:** That's over your allowance of 200. You'll need to pay duty on the excess.

**You:** I see. How much will that be?

**Staff:** Let me calculate... it comes to £35.

**You:** Okay, I'll pay it. Can I pay by card?

**Staff:** Yes, at the desk over there.

**You:** Thank you. Is there anything else?

**Staff:** No, once you've paid you're free to go.

**You:** Understood — I'll go pay now. Sorry for the trouble.

## 14. 听不懂时的万能应对句

- **Sorry, could you say that again?** — 请求重复。
- **What does 'duty' mean?** — 问术语。
- **What should I do with it?** — 问处理。
- **Could you explain what I need to do?** — 求助理解。
- **Did you say I need to pay or leave it?** — 确认要求。

## 15. 英国本地表达

- 英式 `pay duty` = 补关税；`duty-free` 是免税店。
- `come to £35` = 总共 35 英镑（`it comes to` = 合计）。
- `that's fair` = 合理/公平（接受处理时）。
- `sorry for the trouble` = 抱歉添麻烦。

## 16. 易错点

### 易错 1：慌张抗拒

配合是最好策略。`Sure, no problem` 打开包，通常很快就过。

### 易错 2：听不懂乱答

听不懂就问，别乱点头。`Sorry, could you say that again?`

### 易错 3：解释不清物品

用简单词描述：`It's for my personal use` / `It's a gift` / `It's my medicine`。

### 易错 4：不敢问后果

补税、没收都不可怕——问清楚：`What should I do with it?`

### 易错 5：开箱手忙脚乱

慢慢来，配合打开就好。官员见过无数旅客，正常操作。

## 17. 扩展说明

### 17.1 检查权

海关（Border Force）有权随机检查、开箱、询问。配合是义务，也是最快通过的方式。

### 17.2 补税 vs 没收

超额物品通常**补税**；违禁品（禁带食品等）通常**没收**。配合处理一般不升级为罚款。

### 17.3 个人药品

处方药建议带处方/说明，含管制成分的要随身。诚实说明 `It's my personal medication with a prescription`。
