# Scenario English — Pattern Bank

> 本文件维护跨场景复用的**高频可迁移句型**。场景课不再把每一课都当成 10 个全新句型，而是区分 `New Patterns` 与 `Review Patterns`。

## 1. 使用原则

1. Pattern 必须能在多个旅行场景中复用。
2. 优先选择 A2–B1 学习者容易生产的表达。
3. 一个 Core Scene 建议只新增 **3–5 个 Pattern**。
4. Exception / Extension Scene 原则上不新增 Pattern，除非确有必要。
5. 同一个 Pattern 可以有多个自然变体，但只保留一个稳定 ID。
6. Pattern ID 一旦发布不复用。

## 2. Pattern 分类

### A. 发起请求与说明任务

#### P001 — I'd like to + action

`I'd like to + 动作.`

- I'd like to check in.
- I'd like to buy a ticket to Oxford.
- I'd like to change my booking.

**用途：**值机、酒店、买票、预约、客服。

#### P002 — Could I have + noun, please?

`Could I have + 名词, please?`

- Could I have a window seat, please?
- Could I have the bill, please?
- Could I have another towel, please?

**用途：**机场、餐厅、酒店、咖啡店。

#### P003 — Could you + action, please?

`Could you + 动作, please?`

- Could you check that for me, please?
- Could you write that down, please?
- Could you show me where it is, please?

**用途：**几乎所有服务场景。

#### P004 — Can I + action?

`Can I + 动作?`

- Can I pay by card?
- Can I leave my bag here?
- Can I change this ticket?

**用途：**快速确认规则或许可。

#### P005 — Is it possible to + action?

`Is it possible to + 动作?`

- Is it possible to change rooms?
- Is it possible to get an earlier train?

**用途：**请求替代方案，语气比直接要求更柔和。

### B. 确认规则、流程与下一步

#### P006 — Do I need to + action?

`Do I need to + 动作?`

- Do I need to collect my bag in London?
- Do I need to book in advance?
- Do I need to tap out here?

**用途：**机场、交通、景点、酒店。

#### P007 — Where do I need to + action?

`Where do I need to + 动作?`

- Where do I need to go next?
- Where do I need to collect my ticket?

**用途：**流程型场景。

#### P008 — What do I need to + action?

`What do I need to + 动作?`

- What do I need to show at the gate?
- What do I need to bring?

**用途：**证件、材料、预约。

#### P009 — What happens if + condition?

`What happens if + 情况?`

- What happens if I miss the train?
- What happens if my flight is cancelled?

**用途：**规则和异常预判。

#### P010 — Is + noun + included?

`Is + 名词 + included?`

- Is breakfast included?
- Is baggage included?
- Is service included?

**用途：**酒店、机票、餐厅、景点。

### C. 询问地点与方向

#### P011 — Where is + place?

`Where is + 地点?`

- Where is Platform 4?
- Where is baggage reclaim?

#### P012 — Could you tell me where + place + is?

`Could you tell me where + 地点 + is?`

- Could you tell me where the nearest toilet is?

**用途：**更完整、礼貌的问路表达。

#### P013 — Is this the right + noun + for + destination?

`Is this the right + 名词 + for + 地点?`

- Is this the right platform for Oxford?
- Is this the right bus for Camden?

**用途：**交通确认。

#### P014 — Does this + transport + go to + place?

`Does this + 交通工具 + go to + 地点?`

- Does this train go to Edinburgh?

#### P015 — How do I get to + place?

`How do I get to + 地点?`

- How do I get to Terminal 5?
- How do I get to the city centre?

---

### D. 确认听到的信息

#### P016 — Did you say + information?

`Did you say + 信息?`

- Did you say Gate 36?
- Did you say 10:20?
- Did you say £65?

**用途：**数字、时间、地址、费用；高频救场 Pattern。

#### P017 — Was that A or B?

`Was that + A + or + B?`

- Was that 10:20 or 10:30?
- Was that Platform 13 or 30?

#### P018 — So, just to confirm, + statement?

`So, just to confirm, + 陈述?`

- So, just to confirm, I don't need to collect my bag in London?

**用途：**重要信息二次确认。

#### P019 — Could you say that again?

`Could you say that again, please?`

**用途：**通用 conversation repair。

#### P020 — Could you say that more slowly?

`Could you say that a little more slowly, please?`

**用途：**听力压力高时主动降速。

#### P021 — What does + word + mean?

`What does + 词 + mean?`

- What does “off-peak” mean?

#### P022 — Could you say that in a different way?

`Could you say that in a different way?`

**用途：**单纯重复仍听不懂时。

### E. 价格、费用与支付

#### P023 — How much is + noun?

`How much is + 名词?`

- How much is the excess baggage charge?
- How much is a return ticket?

#### P024 — How much does it cost to + action?

`How much does it cost to + 动作?`

- How much does it cost to change the booking?

#### P025 — Can I pay by + method?

`Can I pay by + 支付方式?`

- Can I pay by card?
- Can I pay contactless?

#### P026 — Has this already been charged?

`Has this already been charged?`

**用途：**重复扣款、押金、预授权。

#### P027 — Could I get a receipt, please?

`Could I get a receipt, please?`

**用途：**购物、餐厅、酒店、交通。

### F. 偏好、选择与替代方案

#### P028 — I'd prefer + noun, if possible

`I'd prefer + 名词, if possible.`

- I'd prefer an aisle seat, if possible.
- I'd prefer a quieter room, if possible.

#### P029 — Is there any + noun + condition?

`Is there any + 名词 + 条件?`

- Is there any seat closer to the front?
- Is there any later train?

#### P030 — Do you have anything + adjective?

`Do you have anything + 形容词?`

- Do you have anything cheaper?
- Do you have anything vegetarian?
- Do you have anything smaller?

#### P031 — What would you recommend?

`What would you recommend?`

**用途：**餐厅、景点、购物、药房。

#### P032 — I'll take + noun

`I'll take + 名词.`

- I'll take the 10:30 train.
- I'll take this one.

**用途：**完成选择。

### G. 描述问题与寻求解决

#### P033 — There's a problem with + noun

`There's a problem with + 名词.`

- There's a problem with my booking.
- There's a problem with the room.
- There's a problem with my card.

#### P034 — I think + problem statement

`I think + 问题陈述.`

- I think I've been charged twice.
- I think this is the wrong room.

#### P035 — I was supposed to + action

`I was supposed to + 动作.`

- I was supposed to get a double room.
- I was supposed to arrive at 8:30.

**用途：**描述预期与现实不一致。

#### P036 — I haven't received + noun

`I haven't received + 名词.`

- I haven't received my luggage.
- I haven't received the confirmation email.

#### P037 — Could you check + noun + for me?

`Could you check + 名词 + for me?`

- Could you check the booking for me?
- Could you check the payment for me?

#### P038 — What are my options?

`What are my options?`

**用途：**航班取消、退款、房间问题、交通中断等。

#### P039 — Is there another option?

`Is there another option?`

**用途：**拒绝当前方案并继续推进。

#### P040 — I'd like to + desired resolution

`I'd like to + 期望解决方案.`

- I'd like to change rooms.
- I'd like a refund.
- I'd like to rebook for tomorrow.

### H. 身体状况与紧急信息

#### P041 — I have + symptom

`I have + 症状.`

- I have a sore throat.
- I have a rash.

#### P042 — I've had + symptom + for + duration

`I've had + 症状 + for + 时间.`

- I've had a fever for two days.

#### P043 — It hurts here

`It hurts here.`

**用途：**无法准确说出身体部位时配合指示。

#### P044 — I'm allergic to + noun

`I'm allergic to + 名词.`

- I'm allergic to peanuts.
- I'm allergic to penicillin.

#### P045 — I need help

`I need help.`

**用途：**紧急情况下优先清晰，不追求复杂表达。

### I. 时间、时长与截止

#### P046 — How long does it take to + action?

`How long does it take to + 动作?`

- How long does it take to get through security?
- How long does it take to walk to Gate 42?
- How long does it take to get to the city centre?

**用途：**安检、转机、步行距离、交通、办事流程。

#### P047 — What time do I need to + action?

`What time do I need to + 动作?`

- What time do I need to be at the gate?
- What time do I need to check out?
- What time do I need to drop the bag off?

**用途：**登机、退房、还车、预约、截止时间。

#### P048 — Do you know how long it'll be?

`Do you know how long it'll be?`

- The board says delayed — do you know how long it'll be?
- Do you know how long the queue will be?
- Do you know how long the wait is?

**用途：**延误、排队、等餐、等车、等结果。

#### P049 — Will I have enough time to + action?

`Will I have enough time to + 动作?`

- My connection is 50 minutes — will I have enough time to make it?
- Will I have enough time to get something to eat?
- Will I have enough time to go through passport control?

**用途：**转机、换乘、中途购物用餐、赶末班车。

### J. 规则、限制与是否允许

#### P050 — Am I allowed to + action?

`Am I allowed to + 动作?`

- Am I allowed to take this through security?
- Am I allowed to keep my laptop in the bag?
- Am I allowed to bring food on board?

**用途：**安检、机上、边检、场馆、住宿的允许范围。

#### P051 — Is there a limit on + noun?

`Is there a limit on + 名词?`

- Is there a limit on liquids?
- Is there a limit on hand baggage?
- Is there a limit on how much I can carry?

**用途：**液体、行李、数量、金额、时长的上限。

#### P052 — Does this count as + noun?

`Does this count as + 名词?`

- Does this count as hand baggage?
- Does this count as a personal item?
- Does this count as one bag?

**用途：**行李归类、票种归类、优惠资格判断。

#### P053 — Do I need to take + noun + out?

`Do I need to take + 名词 + out?`

- Do I need to take my laptop out?
- Do I need to take my liquids out of the bag?
- Do I need to take my belt off?

**用途：**安检开包、脱衣物、取出电子设备。

### K. 位置、顺序与流程状态

#### P054 — Am I in the right queue for + noun?

`Am I in the right queue for + 名词?`

- Am I in the right queue for bag drop?
- Am I in the right queue for e-gates?
- Am I in the right queue for Group 3?

**用途：**排错队时快速纠正，避免白等。

#### P055 — Which gate / desk / lane do I need?

`Which + gate / desk / lane / platform + do I need?`

- Which gate do I need for the Edinburgh flight?
- Which desk do I need for oversized baggage?
- Which platform do I need for Oxford?

**用途：**登机口、柜台、通道、站台的定位。

#### P056 — Has + noun + been called yet?

`Has + 名词 + been called yet?`

- Has my flight been called yet?
- Has boarding been called yet?
- Has Group 2 been called yet?

**用途：**确认广播进度、是否已开始登机。

#### P057 — Is this the right + noun + for me?

`Is this the right + 名词 + for me?`

- Is this the right gate for BA1234?
- Is this the right seat for me?
- Is this the right form for me?

**用途：**登机口、座位、表格、车厢的确认。

### L. 求助、协商与升级处理

#### P058 — Could you help me with + noun?

`Could you help me with + 名词?`

- Could you help me with the machine, please?
- Could you help me with my bag, please?
- Could you help me with this form, please?

**用途：**自助设备、行李、表格、语言障碍时求助。

#### P059 — Is there someone who can + action?

`Is there someone who can + 动作?`

- Is there someone who can check this for me?
- Is there someone who can speak Chinese?
- Is there someone who can open the gate?

**用途：**当前工作人员无法处理时请求升级或转介。

#### P060 — Could you check that for me, please?

`Could you check + 内容 + for me, please?`

- Could you check my booking for me, please?
- Could you check if my bag is on the flight?
- Could you check the next available flight for me, please?

**用途：**订位、行李、余位、时间的核查请求。

#### P061 — I've been waiting for + 时长

`I've been waiting for + 时长 + (and ...)`

- I've been waiting for over an hour.
- I've been waiting for 40 minutes and no one has come.
- I've been waiting here since 9 o'clock.

**用途：**礼貌但明确地表达等待过久，推动处理。

#### P062 — Who should I speak to about + noun?

`Who should I speak to about + 名词?`

- Who should I speak to about a missed connection?
- Who should I speak to about compensation?
- Who should I speak to about my luggage?

**用途：**不知道归哪个部门管时定位对口人。

### M. 突发情况与后果确认

#### P063 — What happens if + 情况?

`What happens if + 主语 + 动词?`

- What happens if I miss my connection?
- What happens if the flight is cancelled?
- What happens if my bag is overweight?

**用途：**提前确认后果，用于异常与风险场景。

#### P064 — I think I'm going to miss + noun

`I think I'm going to miss + 名词`

- I think I'm going to miss my connection.
- I think I'm going to miss the last train.
- I think I'm going to miss boarding.

**用途：**主动预警，让工作人员优先处理你。

#### P065 — Is there anything you can do?

`Is there anything you can do (about + 名词)?`

- Is there anything you can do about the seats?
- Is there anything you can do to get me on an earlier flight?
- Is there anything you can do to help?

**用途：**规则不利时的最后一次礼貌协商。

#### P066 — Could you put me on + noun?

`Could you put me on + 名词?`

- Could you put me on the next flight?
- Could you put me on the standby list?
- Could you put me on an earlier train?

**用途：**改签、候补、换班次。

#### P067 — Will I be + past participle?

`Will I be + 过去分词 (compensated / rebooked / refunded / provided with ...)?`

- Will I be charged for that?
- Will I be covered if the flight is cancelled?
- Will I be rebooked on the next flight?

**用途：**费用、赔偿、改签、覆盖范围等被动结果。

#### P068 — I'm here on + 目的 / I'm a + 身份

`I'm here on + 目的 (holiday / business / a visit)`

- I'm here on holiday.
- I'm here on business.
- I'm visiting my family.
- I'm a student / a teacher / I work in a bank.

**用途：**回答来访目的、职业身份——边境问询、酒店入住、社交场合高频。

- Will I be rebooked automatically?
- Will I be provided with a hotel?
- Will I be refunded for the ticket?

**用途：**取消、延误、超售后的权益确认。

## 3. Scene 使用方式

每个 Scene 顶部 metadata 中记录：

```yaml
new_patterns:
  - P001
  - P016
review_patterns:
  - P003
  - P019
```

正文中不需要重复解释所有 Review Pattern，只需：

- 给出本场景中的真实例句；
- 在 role-play 中重复使用；
- 在练习中要求迁移。

## 4. 新增 Pattern 的门槛

新增一个 Pattern 前，先检查：

- 是否已有 Pattern 只需替换名词/动词即可表达？
- 是否至少能在 3 个不同场景中复用？
- 是否比现有简单表达明显更有价值？
- 是否适合 A2–B1 学习者主动说出口？

如果答案大多为否，优先把它当作“场景表达”，不要升级为全局 Pattern。

## 5. Scene 001 初始映射

Scene 001 建议：

**New Patterns**
- P001 — I'd like to + action
- P006 — Do I need to + action?
- P016 — Did you say + information?
- P023 — How much is + noun?
- P028 — I'd prefer + noun, if possible

**Review / Supporting Patterns**
- P002 — Could I have + noun, please?
- P003 — Could you + action, please?
- P018 — So, just to confirm...
- P020 — Could you say that more slowly?
- P029 — Is there any + noun + condition?

后续场景优先复用这些 Pattern，而不是重新创造同义句型。
